Why RTOs choose ParallaxOS

The whole AVETMISS lifecycle in one sovereign system.

From intake to lodgement: capture students once, run enrolments and outcomes, then generate a validated NCVER-spec submission — instead of stitching it together across spreadsheets the week reporting is due.

NAT

All ten AVETMISS NAT files

NAT00010 through NAT00130 — organisation, locations, programs, subjects, clients, postal, disability, prior education, training activity and completions — generated and packed as a UTF-8 ZIP to the NCVER specification.

✓

AVAS validation before you lodge

Tier 1 and Tier 2 AVAS rules run across every file before download, so mandatory-field gaps, invalid codes and logic inconsistencies are caught on screen — not bounced back by the portal after submission.

USI

Student lifecycle & USI checks

Students, enrolments and completions in one register, with NAT00080–NAT00100 demographics captured at intake and USI format verification built in. Competent outcomes auto-create the Statement of Attainment record (NAT00130).

◷

Scope & trainer currency

Track your scope of registration (units and qualifications, ASQA status), and keep trainer/assessor TAE plus industry-currency evidence against the Standards for RTOs 2015 (cl. 1.13–1.16).

⚑

Audit-ready, network-aware

One timeline for ASQA audits and rail network-operator approvals — ARTC, Sydney Trains, Metro Trains, Aurizon and more — with findings and rectification status tracked in place.

⇄

The cross-tenant compliance moat

When you mark a rail worker competent, that completion can flow straight to the contractors who employ them — closing the loop between training delivered and the RIW competencies their scheduler checks.

AU

Sovereign & encrypted

Hosted in AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2). Student personal information is encrypted at rest, with seven-year archival of submission ZIPs and portal acceptance evidence per NCVER retention requirements.

✦

Documents, forms & AI

A document hub with TAS, assessment-tool, validation and complaints templates, a forms catalogue, and the same plain-English AI assistant that powers the rest of the platform.

The submission, end to end

Ten NAT files,
one validated ZIP.

AVETMISS is the standard every RTO reports against to NCVER and the state training authorities. ParallaxOS builds the complete NAT file set from the data you already keep — then validates it before you lodge.

The same records drive your Statements of Attainment and, for rail workers, the competency completions your contractor clients rely on.
